# Pragati (working name): Property Appreciation Intelligence for India

Give it an area or a building and it estimates how much the property is likely
to appreciate over the next 1–5 years, based on the infrastructure and
development projects announced around it. Every forecast is explained, linked
to government and trusted sources, and back-tested.

## Founder's summary

- **Does this already exist in India?** Partly. Portals (99acres, MagicBricks,
  Housing.com, Square Yards, NoBroker) show backward-looking asking-price
  trends. Research firms (Liases Foras, PropEquity, Anarock, Knight Frank, JLL)
  publish city-level reports. Registration-data players (Square Yards, Zapkey,
  PropStack) sell transaction history. **Nobody offers forward-looking,
  building-level forecasts that are explicitly linked to upcoming developments,
  with published accuracy.** That is the wedge.
- **Positioning:** neutral (no selling leads to developers), explainable, and
  source-linked.
- **Business model:** B2C freemium (paid reports + Pro subscription) to build
  brand and data, then B2B and API (developers, banks/HFCs/NBFCs, funds) for
  venture scale.
- **Roadmap:** Day 1 = one city, hand-curated catalysts, map, analyst-assisted
  reports. Day 90 = real models, explainable forecasts, payments, B2B design
  partners. Day 360 = 6–8 cities, completion-probability model, B2B console
  and API, land/plot forecasts.
- **Funding path:** grants/angels, then pre-seed (~₹1–4 Cr) on one-city
  traction, then seed ($1.5–4M) on multi-city revenue and proven accuracy.
  Pitch it as *data infrastructure / lender risk*, not only "proptech".
