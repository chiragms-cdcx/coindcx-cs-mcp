# 03 — Business Model & VC Fundraising Plan

All numbers below are **planning hypotheses**, not market facts. Validate
them with pilots before putting them in a deck.

## 1. Core principle: neutrality is the product

Indian portals make most of their money from developers and brokers, so their
"insights" are not trusted. **We do not sell buyer leads to developers or
brokers in Years 1–2.** Revenue comes from people who need the *truth*:
buyers, investors, lenders and the developers' own land teams.

## 2. Customer segments & revenue streams

| Segment | Pain | Product | Pricing (hypothesis) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home buyers (end users) | "Am I overpaying? Will this area grow?" | Free locality score; paid detailed report | Free tier; ₹499–₹1,999 per report |
| Retail investors & NRIs | Picking the next growth corridor remotely | Pro subscription: unlimited reports, alerts, portfolio, compare | ₹999/month or ₹7,999/year; NRI tier ~$15/month |
| Brokers / channel partners / wealth advisors | Need credible, branded material to advise clients | White-label reports, client portfolios | ₹5k–₹25k/month per seat bundle |
| Developers (land & strategy teams) | Site selection, pricing, launch timing | B2B console: heatmaps, micro-market forecasts, supply pipeline | ₹10–50 lakh/year per developer |
| Banks, HFCs, NBFCs | Collateral valuation and forward risk on loan books | Bulk/API valuation + forecast + risk flags | Per-call (₹20–₹200) + annual platform fee ₹25 lakh–₹1 Cr |
| Funds, AIFs, REITs, family offices | Deal sourcing, underwriting | Data API, custom research | ₹15–75 lakh/year |
| Government / metro corporations (later) | Value-capture financing, TOD planning | Custom analytics | Project-based |

**Revenue mix target:** Year 1 ≈ 60% B2C/prosumer + 40% B2B pilots; Year 3 ≈
25% B2C + 75% B2B/API. B2C builds the brand, data feedback loop and
distribution. B2B builds revenue scale and the venture-scale story.

## 3. Unit economics (targets to validate)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| B2C CAC (content/SEO-led) | < ₹300 per paying user |
| Report gross margin | > 85% once automated |
| Pro subscriber LTV | ₹6,000–₹10,000 (12–15 month retention) |
| B2B ACV | ₹15–50 lakh |
| B2B sales cycle | 3–6 months (developers), 6–12 months (banks) |
| Data cost | Largest COGS line: licensed transaction data plus analyst review |

## 4. Go-to-market

1. **SEO and content moat**: one auto-generated, high-quality page per project, locality and catalyst (100k+ pages). Searches like "Pune metro line 3 impact on Hinjewadi property prices" have intent and little good content.
2. **"Catalyst tracker" newsletter and WhatsApp channel**: weekly government infrastructure updates per city. Cheap audience building.
3. **YouTube / Instagram explainers**: "This ₹12,000 cr expressway will change these 5 localities."
4. **Broker and wealth-advisor partnerships**: they distribute our reports to their clients (white-label).
5. **NRI communities** (Gulf, US, UK): remote due diligence is a strong need.
6. **B2B founder-led sales**: start with 2–3 mid-size developers and one HFC as design partners. Offer free pilots in exchange for case studies and data.

## 5. Moats (what investors will probe)

1. **Curated catalyst graph**: years of structured, lifecycle-tracked government project data with source provenance. Hard and boring to replicate.
2. **Historical impact dataset**: learned uplift curves from hundreds of past Indian infrastructure projects.
3. **Published track record**: back-tested and live forecast accuracy. Trust compounds over time.
4. **Neutrality brand**: portals can't copy this without hurting their own revenue model.
5. **Distribution**: SEO page network + B2B integrations (API embedded in lender workflows is sticky).

## 6. How to get VC funding

### 6.1 Stage plan

| Stage | When | What you need to show | Typical size (India, indicative) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bootstrapped / grants | Day 0–90 | MVP in one city, waitlist, first paid reports | Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (via incubators), state startup grants, angel cheques |
| Pre-seed | ~Day 90–180 | Working forecasts in 1 city, 1–2 B2B pilots, early revenue, strong team | ₹1–4 Cr (~$150k–$500k) |
| Seed | ~Month 12–18 | 4–8 cities, ₹25–50 lakh MRR or strong B2B pipeline, back-test accuracy, retention data | $1.5–4M |
| Series A | ~Month 24–36 | ₹5–10 Cr+ ARR, B2B/API majority, multiple lenders in production | $8–15M |

### 6.2 Who to approach
- **Angel networks & syndicates**: Indian Angel Network, LetsVenture, Venture Catalysts, Inflection Point Ventures, and real-estate operators who angel-invest (they bring developer access).
- **Pre-seed / seed funds**: early-stage funds with a B2B SaaS, fintech or data thesis, e.g. Blume Ventures, 100X.VC, Better Capital, Antler India, Titan Capital, Java Capital, Kae Capital, Chiratae, Prime Venture Partners.
- **Larger seed / Series A**: Accel, Elevation, Peak XV (Surge), Lightspeed, Matrix/Z47, Nexus. Lead with a fintech/lender-risk angle; the "proptech" label alone has been a harder sell in India.
- **Strategics**: proptech arms of developers, housing finance companies, and global proptech funds (e.g. Fifth Wall, MetaProp) once you have traction.
- **Accelerators**: Y Combinator, Antler, Surge, NASSCOM 10,000 Startups, and IIT/IIM incubators (also a route to SISFS grants).

Check each fund's current thesis and portfolio for conflicts (e.g. they back a listing portal) before pitching.

### 6.3 Narrative for the deck (12 slides)
1. **Problem**: Indians put most of their wealth into real estate and buy with almost no forward-looking data. ₹1–2 Cr decisions are made on broker opinions.
2. **Insight**: Government infrastructure is the biggest driver of appreciation, and it is *public* but unstructured (PDFs, tenders, gazettes).
3. **Solution**: Explainable, source-linked, back-tested appreciation forecasts at building level.
4. **Demo**: report page and catalyst map.
5. **Why now**: RERA, IGR digitisation, Gati Shakti infra pipeline, LLM document extraction.
6. **Market**: Indian residential sales are worth several lakh crore rupees a year (use a cited source such as Anarock/Knight Frank annual figures); plus outstanding housing and LAP loan books for lenders. Build TAM → SAM → SOM bottom-up from paying segments.
7. **Business model**: B2C freemium → B2B/API.
8. **Traction**: users, paid reports, pilots, LOIs, accuracy metrics.
9. **Competition**: the map from `01-market-landscape.md`, positioning on two axes: *backward vs forward-looking* × *conflicted vs neutral*.
10. **Moat**: catalyst graph, impact dataset, track record.
11. **Team**: real-estate domain + data/ML + GTM. A credible domain co-founder (ex-research firm, ex-developer land team, ex-lender risk) matters a lot here.
12. **Ask & use of funds**: e.g. 18–24 month runway; ~45% engineering/data, ~25% data acquisition & analysts, ~20% GTM, ~10% legal/ops.

### 6.4 Metrics VCs will ask for
- Forecast accuracy (MAPE / hit-rate within ±X%) by city, back-tested and live.
- Free → paid conversion, report repeat rate, Pro retention.
- B2B pilot → paid conversion, ACV, sales cycle length.
- Data coverage: % of RERA projects covered, number of catalysts tracked, freshness (days from government announcement to our database).
- CAC by channel, SEO traffic growth.

### 6.5 Red flags to pre-empt
| Investor concern | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Forecasts can be wrong, so liability" | Ranges + confidence, published accuracy, information-not-advice terms, legal review |
| "Portals will copy you" | Neutrality + track record + catalyst graph; and portals could become API customers |
| "Data access is fragile" | Multiple sources per field, licensed feeds, RTI, government MoUs, own historical archive |
| "Consumer real estate is infrequent" | Recurring B2B/API revenue; portfolio tracking & alerts for owners keep B2C engaged |
| "Is the market venture-scale?" | Lender/insurer risk analytics and API (US analogues: HouseCanary, CoreLogic) |
